What lives on the desk

Four tools, each built for its job.

The board

Drag projects across pending, in process, done. Slipped due dates show in red, and the activity feed remembers who moved what.

Media rooms

Every project keeps its clips and stills together. Stream in place, download with a progress bar, rename without breaking a single link.

Scripts

One markdown file per script — plus a frozen copy the moment it hits Review or Final. The approved cut always exists.

Viewer links

Share a link, not a password. Clients and viewers see the board read-only; revoke them in one click when the job wraps.
