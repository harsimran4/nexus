Nexus — Privacy Policy

Nexus is a content-management tool for our team. It has no server of its own.

What data Nexus handles

  • Your Google Drive files. Nexus reads and writes files in the specific Drive folder our team shared with it — nothing else in your Google account. The access Google grants is limited to the drive.file scope: files created or opened by Nexus itself.
  • Metadata (project names, item titles, statuses, labels, notes) is stored in a single JSON file inside that same Drive folder. It never leaves Google's infrastructure.
  • Media files (videos, images, documents) are uploaded to that Drive folder and are never copied anywhere else.
  • Login secrets (access tokens or passwords) are stored only as salted one-way hashes inside that same JSON file. We cannot read them.

What Nexus never does

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No cookies beyond your own browser's local sign-in state.
  • No data leaves Google Drive + your browser. There is no third server.

Revoking access

You can revoke Nexus's access to your Google account at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
